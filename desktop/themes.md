# Make BonzAI feel like your workspace

Choose a palette that is comfortable for your screen and surroundings. Themes change the interface, not your models, prompts or generated output.

## Choose a theme

In Desktop, click the theme name in the sidebar to browse themes, or use its left/right arrows to cycle. In Web, click the theme name, search the gallery or filter **Light** and **Dark**, then choose a preview. You can also try Web themes from the welcome panel.

Your selection is saved locally when storage is available. Web and Desktop keep their own preferences; selecting a theme in one does not change the other.
